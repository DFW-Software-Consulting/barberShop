Our story

A shop that still takes its time

The Barber Spot opened in 2012 with two chairs and a simple idea: a good haircut should feel unhurried. Today we are a three-barber shop with the same rule. We book real time for every client, keep the shop clean and the conversation easy, and sweat the small details like edges, blending and the last pass with a razor.

  • Licensed, experienced barbers
  • Sanitized tools between every client
  • Walk-ins welcome, appointments preferred
